about=QAmbit brings openambit2 into QGIS. It syncs a Suunto Ambit (Ambit 1/2/3, Traverse) through openambit2's openambit-cli: new moves, watch time and GPS orbit (no Movescount account); it edits and writes sport modes, including air sports templates, and training plans. Moves are kept in a GeoPackage and shown with QGIS's own tools: tracks styled by activity, 3D views (air sports open in 3D automatically), the elevation profile, and the time slider to replay a move. Terrain comes from AWS Terrain Tiles, IGN LiDAR HD (50 cm, France) or the Copernicus DEM; basemaps come from the HCMGIS plugin. Requires openambit2's openambit-cli (https://github.com/YannChemin/openambit2) and read/write access to the watch's hidraw device.
